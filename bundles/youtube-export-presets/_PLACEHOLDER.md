# YouTube Export Presets

Place your `.epr` export preset files here. The manifest expects:
- MCM_YT_1080p.epr
- MCM_YT_4K.epr
- MCM_YT_Shorts.epr

To create export presets in Premiere Pro: open the Export dialog, configure your settings, click the small "save preset" icon next to the Preset dropdown, then locate the saved `.epr` file in your Premiere Pro EPR folder and copy it here.
