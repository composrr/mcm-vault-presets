# Signature PowerGrades

Place your `.drx` PowerGrade files here. The manifest expects:
- MCM_Signature_Warm.drx
- MCM_Signature_Cool.drx
- MCM_Signature_Bleach.drx

To export a PowerGrade as DRX: in Resolve's Color page, right-click a still in your Gallery and choose "Export". Save with the .drx extension.

NOTE: This bundle is installType: manual. Files will sync to the user's local folder, and the app shows import instructions inside Resolve.
